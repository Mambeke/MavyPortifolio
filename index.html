Who I am

Financial understanding and data analysis, brought together.

My training includes ALX Africa's Data Science programme and a professional data analytics certificate. I have also applied these skills during a Data Science & Analytics internship, working on practical business questions involving sales performance, customer segmentation and conversion analysis.

Alongside data analytics, I am studying towards the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) qualification to strengthen my understanding of financial reporting, accounting principles and business performance. This combination helps me look beyond charts and consider what the figures mean in a financial and operational context.

The aim is not simply to produce another spreadsheet or dashboard. It is to make the information useful: explain what the data shows, where attention may be needed, and which questions a business should investigate next. Deliverables are designed to be understandable and practical for the people using them.

What I fix

When business data is available but the answers are not.

Many organisations collect sales, expense, customer and stock information across different systems and spreadsheets. Bringing that information together can make it easier to understand performance and spot issues that deserve attention.

Reporting takes too much manual work

When reports depend on repeated copying and manual calculations, they take time to prepare and are harder to refresh consistently.

📉

Sales do not tell the whole story

Revenue alone does not show margins, product costs, customer patterns or which parts of the business contribute most to performance.

🔍

Inconsistent or difficult-to-use data

Duplicate records, inconsistent categories and unclear spreadsheet structures can undermine analysis and make reporting less reliable.

Inventory & Stock Analysis

Know what is moving, what is sitting and what needs attention

Stock affects both cash flow and the ability to meet customer demand. I analyse inventory records and stock movement to help businesses understand purchasing patterns, identify slow-moving and fast-moving items, monitor stock levels and investigate differences between recorded and expected quantities.

What this helps you understandWhich items move quickly, which may be tying up working capital and where stock records or replenishment decisions need review.
  • Stock movement and inventory trend analysis
  • Fast-moving, slow-moving and non-moving stock identification
  • Stock-level monitoring and reorder insights
  • Inventory reconciliation and variance reporting support

Product Profitability Analysis

Look beyond sales to understand product contribution

A product can sell well without contributing as much profit as expected. I bring together available sales, pricing and cost information to assess product-level performance, compare margins and identify products or categories that may require a closer look. The analysis can support pricing, purchasing, promotion and product-range decisions.

What this helps you understandWhich products contribute most to gross profit, where margins are under pressure and how pricing or costs affect product performance.
  • Sales value and gross profit by product or category
  • Gross margin and product contribution comparisons
  • Low-margin and loss-making items flagged for review
  • Pricing, discount and product-mix insights

Accounts Receivable & Payable Analysis

Improve visibility over money owed and money due

Unpaid customer invoices and upcoming supplier payments affect the cash available to run a business. I analyse receivables and payables records to help you understand outstanding balances, how long invoices have remained unpaid, upcoming payment obligations and changes in working-capital patterns. This is analytical reporting support, not debt collection or bookkeeping.

What this helps you understandWho owes the business, which balances are overdue, what payments are approaching and where follow-up may be required.
  • Accounts receivable and payable summaries
  • Invoice ageing analysis and overdue balance reports
  • Customer and supplier balance reviews
  • Payment-pattern and working-capital insights
